The question this answers

How should a learner find their next problem: by the official section, by technique and data shape, or by a suggested order?

Assumption: the 23 official sections mix two different ideas (a data structure such as Stack or Trie, and a technique such as Two Pointers or Backtracking). The technique and structure labels here are a first draft by Claude. Review them before trusting any variant built on them.

A. Sections. Matches the list people already know. Cheap to build. Progress is a count per section.
B. Technique by shape. Shows where one technique appears in different shapes. Needs the labels to be right.
C. Suggested order. Answers "what next" directly. Needs you to decide the order, which is opinion.
